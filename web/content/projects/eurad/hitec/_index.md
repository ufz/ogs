The aim of [EURAD-HITEC](https://www.ejp-eurad.eu/implementation/influence-temperature-clay-based-material-behaviour-hitec) was to develop and document an improved understanding of the thermally-induced hydration of clay-based materials (host rock and buffer) when exposed to elevated temperatures ($>100°C$) over extended periods of time.
